# Math 1201 Review

A generated practice question bank for **Academic Mathematics 1201** (Newfoundland and Labrador), built on the same chassis as the Math 2201, 2200, 3200, 3201, and 3208 Review apps.

**Live app:** https://cdli-nlschools.github.io/Math1201Review/

## What it does

- Draws fresh practice questions by unit or by curriculum indicator, with a level mix following the published 2026-2027 tables of specifications (about 20% Level 1, 60% Level 2, 20% Level 3 in both the mid-course and end-of-course scopes).
- Mid-course preset: Units 1 to 3 (Measurement; Roots and Powers; Factors and Products). Final preset: Units 4 to 7 (Relations and Functions; Linear Functions; Systems of Linear Equations; Trigonometry).
- Three ways to work: practice online with instant checking, print a practice sheet (with working space and an optional answer key), or print a practice exam built to the tables of specifications (27 selected-response marks and 23 constructed-response marks, with a separate teacher key). Sheets and exams can also be downloaded as a Word file with native editable equations, or copied for Google Docs.
- Every question carries a full worked solution.

## Sources and originality

Items are generated from templates written for this bank, informed by the Academic Mathematics 1201 Curriculum Guide and the provincial tables of specifications. Question types are also informed by a type-level harvest of classroom practice sets, the Drive archive's ExamView banks and 2017 final for this course, and the Math 1201 development shell on Brightspace. No question text, scenario, or values from any source are reproduced.
